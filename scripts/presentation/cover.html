From E. coli K-12

RegulonDB has been the international curated reference for bacterial transcriptional regulation since 1991. This deck walks through the prototype of RegulonDB MG — the redesign that extends curation to Salmonella enterica, Bacillus subtilis, and additional bacterial models, integrating experimental evidence with computational predictions, orthology, metabolic context, and high-throughput datasets.
